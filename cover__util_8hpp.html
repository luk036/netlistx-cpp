Detailed Description

Internal helpers shared by the hypergraph covering algorithms.

Free templates that de-duplicate the "is a net covered?" scans and the reverse-delete post-processing pass used by <netlistx/netlist_algo.hpp>, <netlistx/cover.hpp> and <netlistx/rand_cover.hpp>. They live in netlistx::detail and are not part of the public algorithm API.
